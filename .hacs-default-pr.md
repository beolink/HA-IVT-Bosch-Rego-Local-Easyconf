Adds `beolink/HA-IVT-Bosch-Rego-Local-Easyconf`, an integration for **IVT and Bosch heat pumps** (and the rest of the EMS family behind an EMS-ESP gateway: Buderus, Nefit, Junkers, Worcester), local first.

One heat pump is read through every way in it has, in a fixed order: an EMS-ESP gateway on the pump's EMS bus (BBQKees or any EMS-ESP32 board, over its REST API), a Husdata H60/H66 interface on the Rego controller's bus, and Bosch's cloud (the K30/K40 module, IVT Anywhere II / HomeCom Easy) last, only to fill in what the local sources lack or to stand in while they are away. Set-up searches the local network before asking for any login, and EMS-ESP gateways are discovered by mDNS and DHCP. Every value comes with a Swedish or English name and an explanation; it offers sensors, binary sensors, numbers, selects, switches, a climate entity per heating circuit, a water heater, a heating mode select for energy managers, a coefficient of performance, fault codes in words from the manufacturers' published lists, diagnostics, and a sidebar page that explains each value. English is the base language, Swedish is included as a translation.
